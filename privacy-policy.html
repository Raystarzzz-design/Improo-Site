This Privacy Policy explains how Improo collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website (improo.vercel.app) and the Improo app. By using Improo, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Improo is operated by R. Lousberg. If you have any questions about how your data is handled, you can contact us at contact.improo@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

On the website (improo.vercel.app)

  • Email address — if you sign up for our newsletter or contact us
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type (via analytics tools)
  • Cookie data — see section 7

In the Improo app

  • Account information — name and email address
  • Health data — weight logs, step counts, fasting records, and nutrition data that you choose to enter
  • Community content — posts, comments, and reactions you share on the platform
  • Activity data — points earned, challenges completed, and platform interactions
  • Device information — device type and operating system (for technical support purposes)

Health data (weight, steps, fasting, nutrition) is considered a special category of personal data under GDPR (Article 9). We only process this data based on your explicit consent, which you provide when you choose to use these features.

4. How long we keep your data

  • Account data: retained for as long as your account is active, plus 30 days after deletion to allow for recovery
  • Health data: deleted immediately upon account deletion or upon your request
  • Points and transaction history: individual transaction records are retained for 3 months, after which they are automatically deleted. Aggregated profile data (such as total scores and ranking figures) is retained for as long as your account is active, as it is functionally necessary for the operation of the platform.
  • Newsletter email: retained until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: retained for a maximum of 26 months

5. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We work with the following third-party service providers who may process data on our behalf:

  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Google — app distribution (Google Play), analytics, and account services
  • Stripe — payment processing (website purchases, phase 2)
  • Email provider — newsletter delivery (to be updated)

All providers are bound by data processing agreements and are required to handle your data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — request that inaccurate data be corrected
  • Deletion — request that your data be deleted ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact.improo@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

7. Cookies

The Improo website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device. We use:

  • Functional cookies — necessary for the website to work correctly
  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors use the site (requires your consent)

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. The Improo app does not use browser cookies.

8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page. For significant changes, we will notify registered users via email or an in-app notification. Continued use of the platform after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, contact us at contact.improo@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days.

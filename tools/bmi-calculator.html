BMI is a rough guide — not a verdict

BMI only looks at your height and weight. It can't see how much of your weight is muscle versus fat, where your body stores fat, or how your age affects your build — so it's a useful starting point, not a diagnosis.

A heavily-trained athlete and someone who is sedentary can land on the exact same BMI for very different reasons. Use this number as one data point, not the whole picture.

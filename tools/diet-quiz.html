What this can (and can't) tell you

This is a preference and lifestyle fit-check, not a nutrition prescription. It looks at what you enjoy eating, how strict you're comfortable being, and how you actually live day to day — then matches that against well-documented diet styles.

It can't account for allergies, medical conditions, or goals that need a professional's input. Treat the results as a sensible starting point to research further, not a final answer.

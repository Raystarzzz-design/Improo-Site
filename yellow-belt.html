Why Yellow Belt

Everyone starts at Yellow. It's not easy — showing up every day is harder than it sounds. But Yellow Belt is designed to prove something important to yourself: that you can be consistent, even on the hard days. One missed day is allowed per phase, because life happens. What matters is that you come back. Complete any Yellow Belt phase and you unlock the Green Belt — the next level of structure and challenge.
